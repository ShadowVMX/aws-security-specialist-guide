SC-100 · 48 questions · 100 minutes

Exam simulation

A full exam with the same domain mix as the real one, timed and with no feedback until you submit. It trains what the per-module quizzes cannot: reading long stems under time pressure and budgeting the clock.

About the question count and the score Microsoft does not publish a fixed number: SC-100 usually carries between 40 and 60 questions. This simulation uses 48 in 100 minutes with each domain's official weight. The real score is scaled out of 1000 and you pass at 700, which is not a straight 70% of correct answers; the 70% this simulation marks is a working reference, not the exam's grade.

Questions are drawn at random from the guide's 212 practice questions, so every attempt comes out different.
