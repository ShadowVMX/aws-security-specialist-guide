How it is built

All three guides share one engine and the same guarantees, checked automatically on every change:

  • The domains and their weights come from the official exam guide, not from an interpretation.
  • Every official skill has questions; if one is left without any, the site does not publish.
  • Every correct answer links to the documentation page that backs it, checked against the server to confirm it is alive and on topic.
  • Questions are audited against the shortcuts that let you score without knowing: the answer always in the same position, or the longest option.
  • No real exam dumps. Original content written from public documentation.
